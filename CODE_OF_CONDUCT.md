# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ESP32-OS to be a friendly place for everyone, regardless of age, experience, background, nationality, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, religion or any other personal characteristic. Beginners are explicitly welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, also when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Help others, especially people who are new to embedded development or Rust.
- Admit mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment, trolling or personal attacks
- Discriminating, sexualized or violent language or images
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Spam and repeated off-topic posts
- Any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, commits and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer [@TheUnkn0wnC0DE](https://github.com/TheUnkn0wnC0DE). If you do not want to report publicly, use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) function. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code of conduct, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban contributors depending on how serious and repeated the behavior is.
